# Approval Policy
## Purpose
Define decision authority.
## Scope
All project changes.
## Main Content
Green: autonomous routine fixes, tests, formatting, documentation, safe maintenance, refactoring, and developer experience. Yellow: autonomous implementation with reporting for internal organization, abstractions, maintainability, and non-public configuration. Red: CTO approval before Manifest, Handbook, Architecture Baseline, AI philosophy, decision behavior, approved engine responsibilities, public APIs, breaking changes, major dependencies, or product strategy changes. Red reports require problem, options, pros/cons, recommendation, and explicit approval.
