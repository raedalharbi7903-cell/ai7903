# Success Metrics

## Purpose

Define measurable engineering and governance outcomes without predefining product features.

## Scope

Applies to approved sprint delivery and long-term platform quality.

## Main Content

Near-term measures:

- Approved sprint scope is delivered without unapproved features.
- Applicable tests, formatting, linting, imports, and builds pass.
- Every completed sprint has a transparent Engineering Delivery Report.
- Architecture, policy, and product decisions are versioned documentation.
- Known environment limitations are explicit and tracked separately from software defects.

Long-term measures:

- Analysis capabilities remain deterministic, modular, and explainable.
- Platform changes preserve maintainability and contract clarity.
- Technical debt and operational risk are addressed before they compound.
