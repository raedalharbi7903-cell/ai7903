# Product Philosophy

## Purpose

Define product principles for future approved work.

## Scope

Applies to product behavior and user-facing decisions when those capabilities are approved.

## Main Content

The product must favor clarity, explicit boundaries, and trustworthy explanations over opaque output. It must not present ungrounded analysis as deterministic fact. Product scope grows only through approved sprints; foundations must not imply features that have not been approved.
