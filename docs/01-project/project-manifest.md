# Project Manifest

## Purpose

Define the official identity, boundaries, and enduring commitments of the AI Trading Platform.

## Scope

Applies to product, engineering, documentation, and sprint governance.

## Main Content

The AI Trading Platform is a production-grade technical-analysis platform. Its foundation is deterministic analysis architecture; AI is a future explanation and reasoning layer, not a replacement for deterministic analysis.

In scope: maintainable backend and frontend foundations, analysis-engine framework, governed documentation, testing, validation, and operational quality.

Out of scope until separately approved: authentication, users, trading execution, payments, notifications, dashboards, charts, external APIs, bots, technical indicators, recommendations, and market analysis.

Non-negotiable rules: preserve approved architecture, avoid hidden behavior, keep business logic out of routes, validate work before review, and do not begin a new sprint without CTO approval.
