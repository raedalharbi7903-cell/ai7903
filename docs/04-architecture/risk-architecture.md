# Risk Architecture
## Purpose
Define risk as a distinct analysis responsibility.
## Scope
Future risk context.
## Main Content
Risk may describe invalidation, uncertainty, no-trade conditions, and future stop/target/risk-reward context. It must not invent formulas or position advice. Exact calculations and policies are Decision Required.
