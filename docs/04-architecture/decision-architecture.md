# Decision Architecture
## Purpose
Separate decisions from evidence and explanations.
## Scope
Future decision layer.
## Main Content
Decisions consume validated evidence and scenarios; they are not guaranteed outcomes. They require provenance, uncertainty, explicit rejection/no-decision states, and explanation inputs. Decision-state taxonomy and construction rules are Decision Required.
