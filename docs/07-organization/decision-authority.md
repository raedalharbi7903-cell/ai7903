# Decision Authority
## Purpose
Define standards for Decision Authority.
## Scope
Architecture and governance specification only; no runtime behavior is implemented by this document.
## Main Content
Current named authority is CTO and Engineering ownership as documented. Administrative/site roles cannot be recovered reliably from repository context and are **Historical Confirmation Required**. System permissions and organizational role definitions are Decision Required; no role or permission is invented here.

**Implemented:** documentation only. **Approved Future Architecture:** this subject may be implemented only through approved work. **Decision Required:** exact algorithms, formulas, providers, permissions, and operational thresholds are not approved unless stated otherwise.
